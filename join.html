Work with us

The OD3 Lab trains people who can move between materials science, pharmacology and eye biology. If that sounds like you, we would like to hear from you.

Postdoctoral fellows

Bring an engineering or virology background

We look for experience in drug delivery, biomaterials, pharmaceutics, ocular biology or virology. Send your CV, a short note on your research interests and the names of three references.

Graduate students

Rotations and thesis projects

We host students from GEMS, Biomedical Engineering, Pharmaceutical Sciences and related UIC programs. Email us to discuss a rotation.

Undergraduates

Learn how research works

Motivated undergraduates who can commit at least two semesters and about ten hours a week are welcome. Tell us about your coursework and goals.
