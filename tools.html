qPCR fold change calculator

Enter Ct values for your target and reference (housekeeping) gene. The calculator normalizes each sample to the reference gene, compares it to the mean of your control samples and reports fold change by the 2−ΔΔCt method. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

The 2−ΔΔCt method assumes near 100% amplification efficiency for both genes (Livak and Schmittgen, Methods 2001). Validate primer efficiency before relying on fold changes. To paste, copy four columns from Excel in the order sample, group (control or treated), target Ct, reference Ct.
