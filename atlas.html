      About this data

      How the numbers are made

      Public human RNA-seq studies with uninfected (mock) and infected samples were collected from NCBI SRA. Studies with more than one cell line were split so each cell line is compared only with its own mock samples. Reads were quantified with kallisto and summed to gene level.

      Within each study, genes with very low expression were removed and infected samples were compared with mock samples using DESeq2, with correction for multiple testing (UP or DOWN at adjusted p < 0.05, otherwise ns). Fold changes from all studies of the same pathogen were then pooled with a random-effects model, giving more weight to more reliable studies, and tested against no change, with Bonferroni correction across pathogens (stars).

      Studies differ in cell type and infection conditions, so pooled values describe a general tendency rather than a single experiment.

      Reading the plot

      What you are looking at

      • Each dot is one infected sample, shown as log2 fold change against the mean of its own study's mock samples.
      • Violins show the spread of those samples. The thick line is the median and dashed lines are the quartiles.
      • Stars above each pathogen are the pooled test: * p < 0.05, ** p < 0.01, *** p < 0.001, **** p < 0.0001. Significant pathogens are colored.
      • Click any pathogen to see every study behind it, with TPM values and each study's own DESeq2 result.

      Pathogens: Pseudomonas aeruginosa, Mycobacterium tuberculosis, Candida albicans, Aspergillus fumigatus, HSV-1, CMV, adenovirus, EBV, VZV and Zika virus.
